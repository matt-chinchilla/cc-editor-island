### Placement windows asked of the host, and the selection filter (`selectFilter`)

Island `v0.2.3-cc25`, in a `sch` or `pcb` frame (unit m6-i). Our engine from run 15 asks the host in place of six of KiCad's placement windows, through the prompt host (Prompts, above), and reads and writes KiCad's selection filter with no window.

The placement asks and their records (lengths in millimeters, angles in degrees):

- `moveExact` (Move Exactly, modal): `{ x, y, rotation, anchor, anchors }`. `x` and `y` the move (Cartesian; a polar entry is the card's own), `rotation` the turn, `anchors` the rotation anchors the window offers in its order, from `item`, `selectionCenter`, `userOrigin`, `drillOrigin` (the last in the board editor only), `anchor` the chosen one. Answer `{ x?, y?, rotation?, anchor? }`: an anchor not offered is refused with field `anchor`; a move past KiCad's largest board is refused with field `x` (or `y`) and KiCad's sentence; `rotation` within 3,600 degrees. KiCad keeps the last answer for the next ask.
- `offset` (Interactive Offset, modal, after its two clicks): `{ x, y }`, the measured vector. Answer `{ x?, y? }`.
- `array` (Create Array, modeless): `{ shape, arrangeSelection, uniqueReferences, padNumbering, grid, circular }`. `shape` `grid` or `circular`; `padNumbering` true in the footprint editor only; `grid` `{ nx, ny, dx, dy, offsetX, offsetY, centered, stagger, staggerRows, horizontalThenVertical, reverseNumberingAlternate, numberingStartSpecified, numbering2d, primary, secondary }`; `circular` `{ centerX, centerY, angle, angleOffset, clockwise, count, rotateItems, numberingStartSpecified, axis }` (the center in board coordinates); an axis `{ type, start, step }`, `type` `numeric`, `hex`, `alphaShort` (no I, O, S, Q, X, Z) or `alphaFull`, `start` text, `step` a whole number. Answer any of the record's keys, `grid` and `circular` each with any of their own: a bad key refuses with its own name as field, a bad value inside `grid` or `circular` with field `grid` or `circular`. A given circular `angle` clears KiCad's full circle. An accepted answer makes the array in ONE undo step and ends the prompt with no `ev.promptEnd` (`{ accepted: true }` closes the card). `prompt.pick` `{ kind: "point" }` or `{ kind: "item" }` runs KiCad's own picker for the center; the new record arrives as `ev.promptUpdate`.
- `positionRelative` (Position Relative, modeless): `{ anchor: { kind, x, y, label }, x, y }`. `anchor.kind` `item`, `point`, `gridOrigin` or `userOrigin`, at `x`, `y`, with KiCad's anchor text `label`; `x`, `y` the offset. Answer `{ x?, y?, anchor? }`: `anchor` `gridOrigin` or `userOrigin` alone sets the anchor and keeps the prompt, the new record sent as `ev.promptUpdate`; an answer with `x` or `y` moves the selection (one undo step) and ENDS the prompt, as KiCad 10's OK hides its window (a second move needs the command again). `prompt.pick` `{ kind: "item" }` or `{ kind: "point" }` runs KiCad's own picker; the result arrives as `ev.promptUpdate`.
- `filterSelection` (Filter Selected Items, modal): `{ includeFootprints, includeLockedFootprints, includeTracks, includeVias, includeZones, includeItemsOnTechLayers, includeBoardOutlineLayer, includePcbTexts }`, booleans. Answer any of them, booleans only (a non boolean refused with its key as field); the locked footprints box follows the footprints box, as in KiCad.
- `findFootprint` (Get and Move Footprint, modal): `{ references }`, the references KiCad lists, in its order. Answer `{ reference }`, matched without regard to case; one the list lacks is refused with field `reference`. The footprint then hangs on the pointer (KiCad's move), placed by the next click.

A record of any other shape is not taken, and KiCad's own window shows as before.

- **`selectFilter.get`** (no args): the selection filter of the frame's editor, KiCad's Selection Filter pane's switches: in a `pcb` frame `lockedItems`, `footprints`, `text`, `tracks`, `vias`, `pads`, `graphics`, `zones`, `keepouts`, `dimensions`, `points`, `otherItems`; in a `sch` frame `lockedItems`, `symbols`, `text`, `wires`, `labels`, `pins`, `graphics`, `images`, `ruleAreas`, `otherItems`; each a boolean. A read: answered during a check.
- **`selectFilter.set`** (exactly `{ filter }`): sets the named switches and keeps the others; answered with the filter read back. A switch of the other editor, none at all or a non boolean answers `bad_args` and nothing is sent. No undo step, no modified flag; the filter lives in the engine (each engine holds its own, as KiCad does per editor) and is never saved with the design. `not_applied` when the engine refused the set or the read back does not hold it.
- Failures of both: `bad_args`, `unsupported` (in another frame, "the selection filter is read in a sch or pcb frame", or on an engine without `kicadSelectionFilterGet` or `kicadSelectionFilterSet`, named), `not_ready` (no document open), `busy` (a load parked, a popup menu, a dialog or a prompt up, or a placed item on the pointer).
- `ev.ready` lists `selectFilter` in a `sch` or `pcb` frame exactly when the engine has both exports. On an older engine (run 14 and before) the cap is absent and both ops answer `unsupported` naming `kicadSelectionFilterGet`.
