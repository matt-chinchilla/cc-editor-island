<!-- part: events -->
- `ev.export { job: string, phase: "loading" | "models" | "running", percent?: number }`: the progress of a `project.export` run of `pcb_export_3d` or `pcb_render`, at most every 250 ms, exactly these keys. `loading` while the 3D service's files load (percent of bytes), `models` while the bodies the board's model lines name are staged (percent of files), `running` from the render's `Rendering <n> percent` lines. The same phase and percent is never sent twice in a row.
<!-- part: notes project.export -->
- The 3D outputs (island `v0.2.3-cc25`, engine run 15): the job `pcb_render` (KiCad's raytraced picture of the board, `png` or `jpg`), listed in `island.json` `exportKinds`; a host gates it on `ev.ready` `caps` listing `render` (the engine's `kicadOutputJobs()` lists `pcb_render`).
- Before a `pcb_export_3d` or `pcb_render` run the island stages the bodies the board's shown model lines name: a `${KICAD*_3DMODEL_DIR}` line through the model store, a `${KIPRJMOD}`, `$(KIPRJMOD)` or bare relative line copied from the project folder to `/pcbjam/3dmodels/kiprjmod/<relative path>`; a `kicad-embed://` line needs nothing; an absolute path, another variable or a path leaving the project resolves to nothing. At most 2,000 files and 512 MiB; the lines past them, and the bodies not found, go into the file without a body and KiCad's own warning names them in `notes`.
- Without the cap `occ`, every `pcb_export_3d` runs with `cc_occ: false` added to its settings when the engine's job takes that key (only VRML is made then).
- Each job's time bound comes from `bridge/exportTiming.json` (`island.json` `timing.exportMs`): `pcb_export_3d` and `pcb_render` five times their slowest measured run, at least 60 s and at most 900 s; every other job 45 s.
- Held in a `pcb` frame, each sending `ev.held` and opening nothing: KiCad's File > Export > STEP (`pcbnew.EditorControl.exportSTEP`) and VRML (`pcbnew.EditorControl.exportVRML`) under the cap `export` (the host's Outputs page opens on 3D model for CAD and on VRML), and KiCad's 3D viewer's Export Image (`3DViewer.Control.exportImage`) and Copy 3D image (`3DViewer.Control.copyToClipboard`) under `heldKeys`, with no door of the host's.
