- `ev.schItem { uuid, sheetPath, kind, isNew }`: in a `sch` frame (island `v0.2.3-cc25`, cap `schItem`), KiCad's schematic editor was about to open the window of a schematic item that is not a symbol: E, a double click or Properties on a label, wire, graphic line, bus entry, junction, text, text box, shape, rule area, picture, sheet, sheet pin or group (a label's field asks with the label's uuid), or a drawing tool that just made a label, text, text box, sheet or sheet pin (`isNew` true, its new uuid) or was pressed to place a picture (`kind` `image`, `isNew` true, the uuid `00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000`). No window opens: the item stays selected, a new one goes down as the window's OK would place it (a label named as KiCad proposes, a text "Text", an empty text box, a sheet "Untitled Sheet" with KiCad's next free file name), and the picture tool ends (the host then offers a file and sends `sch.image.place`). `sheetPath` is the sheet shown, in `symbol.set`'s form. A table, a no connect, a field alone and a symbol send no `ev.schItem` (a symbol keeps `ev.properties`). Sent once per request, after `ev.ready`, while a schematic is open.

### Schematic items: sch.item.get, sch.item.set, sch.image.place

Every schematic item that is not a symbol, read and changed with no KiCad window, in a `sch` frame with a schematic open (island `v0.2.3-cc25`, on an engine with `kicadSchItemGet`, `kicadSchItemSet`, `kicadHostSchItems` and `kicadCollabBusy`, engine run 15; a host gates `sch.item.get`, `sch.item.set` and `ev.schItem` on `ev.ready` `caps` listing `schItem`, and `sch.image.place` on `schImagePlace` (the engine's `kicadSchPlaceImage` and `kicadCollabGetSelection`); both listed by `sch` frames only). Positions and lengths are millimeters as the schematic stores them, Y down; colors are `""` (KiCad's default) or `#rrggbbaa`; uuids lower case 8-4-4-4-12; a sheet path is `symbol.set`'s form, or `""` in a request for the sheet shown.

- The checks, in order, every one before any effect: the args (`bad_args`); the frame's kind (`unsupported`, "a schematic edits in a sch frame"); the engine (`not_ready` before `ev.ready`; `unsupported` naming the first missing export); a schematic opened by `project.open` or `project.import` (`not_ready`); a load parked, a popup menu or a dialog up, or an item hanging off the pointer (`busy`, nothing changed).
- **Records.** `SchItemKind` is one of `label`, `wire`, `line`, `busEntry`, `junction`, `text`, `textbox`, `shape`, `ruleArea`, `image`, `sheet`, `sheetPin`, `table`, `tableCell`, `group`. Every record carries `{ kind, uuid, sheetPath }` and exactly its kind's keys:
  - `label`: `labelKind` (`local`, `global`, `hierarchical`, `directive`), `text`, `x`, `y`, `orientation` (`right`, `up`, `left`, `down`), `size`, `bold`, `italic`, `color`, `shape` (null for a local label; `input`, `output`, `bidirectional`, `tri_state`, `passive` for a global or hierarchical one; `dot`, `round`, `diamond`, `rectangle` for a directive), `pinLength` (a directive's flag length, null otherwise), `fields` (at most 32 of `{ name, value, visible, x, y }`: a global label's Intersheetrefs, a directive's Netclass and Component Class).
  - `wire`: `bus` (read only), `points` (`[[x, y], [x, y]]`), `width`, `style` (`default`, `solid`, `dash`, `dot`, `dash_dot`, `dash_dot_dot`), `color`. `line` (a graphic line): the same less `bus`. `busEntry`: `x`, `y`, `size` (`[x, y]`, read only), `width`, `style`, `color`. `junction`: `x`, `y`, `diameter` (0: the default), `color`.
  - `text`, `textbox`, `shape`: the symbol editor's records (`symedit.items.get`) with `unit` and `bodyStyle` 0, plus `sheetPath` and, on a text or text box, `color` (the text's); a text's, text box's or table cell's `text` is read at any length KiCad holds, up to 16,384 characters (KiCad sets none), a record past that refusing the read. `ruleArea`: a shape's keys with the kind `ruleArea`.
  - `image`: `x`, `y`, `scale`, `bytes` (the picture's size as KiCad keeps it, read only), `png` (its base64, null past 64 KiB).
  - `sheet`: `name`, `file` (relative to the file of the sheet that holds it), `page`, `x`, `y`, `size` (`[w, h]`), `borderWidth`, `borderColor`, `fillColor`, `inBom`, `onBoard`, `inSim`, `dnp`, `fields` (its user fields, at most 32 of `{ name, value, visible }`), `pins` (a count, read only).
  - `sheetPin`: `sheet` (its sheet's uuid), `name`, `shape` (a port shape), `side` (`left`, `right`, `top`, `bottom`), `size`, `x`, `y`.
  - `table` (read only here): `rows`, `cols`, `cells` (every cell's text, row by row), `borderExternal`, `borderHeader`, `border` (`{ width, style, color }`), `separatorsRows`, `separatorsCols`, `separators`, `colWidths`, `rowHeights`. `tableCell` (read only here): a text box's keys and `color`, plus `table` (its table's uuid), `row`, `col`, `rowSpan`, `colSpan`.
  - `group`: `name`, `members` (a count), `designBlock` (its design block's id, `""` for none).
- **`sch.item.get`** answers the records of 1 to 512 items asked, in the order asked; an item the schematic does not hold is left out. With `sheetPath` `""` the item on the sheet shown, else on the first path holding it. A read: answered while a `sheet.check` runs, no undo step.
- **`sch.item.set`** sets `set`, a non empty partial of one kind's editable keys, on 1 to 4,096 items (no uuid twice), all of `kind`. Editable: a label's every key but `kind`, `uuid` and `sheetPath`, its `fields` as at most 32 `{ name, value?, visible? }` (a named field updated, another name a user field added); a wire's and a line's `points`, `width`, `style`, `color`; a bus entry's `width`, `style`, `color`; a junction's `diameter`, `color`; a text's, text box's, shape's and rule area's keys as `symedit.items.set` takes them (`unit` and `bodyStyle` only 0) and a text's or text box's `color`; a picture's `x`, `y`, `scale` (0.001 to 1,000) and `png` (base64 of a PNG or JPEG of at most 4 MiB, which replaces it: `too_large` past it); a sheet's every key but `pins`, `fields` as a label's; a sheet pin's `name`, `shape`, `side`, `size`; a group's `name`. A table or a table cell is never set here (`bad_args`, "a table is read only here"). Text is at most 1,024 characters with no control character, a page number 1 to 64, a size above 0.
  - The items are read first: `not_found` (`message` the uuid) for the first the schematic does not hold, `bad_args` when one is of another kind or the set does not fit it (`message` why: a label shape its kind after the set has no word for, a flag length on a label that is not a directive, a point count its shape does not take, a file change on more than one sheet).
  - `labelKind` runs KiCad's own change label kind: the label keeps its net; KiCad gives the converted label a NEW uuid, listed in `replaced` as `{ from, to }` and answered under it. A label kind or a sheet file changes only on the sheet shown (`bad_args` otherwise).
  - A sheet's `file` (relative, ending `.kicad_sch`, no `..` and no backslash) is KiCad's sheet file change with the page's answer: `fileMode` `useExisting` links the sheet to that file as it is, `rename` saves this sheet's content under the new name and keeps the old file. A `file` that exists (on disk or shown by another sheet) asked without `fileMode` answers `bad_args` "fileMode is required: the file exists", nothing changed; a new name without it is a `rename`. The change is listed in `renamed` and is outside the undo list, as KiCad's own; a step KiCad refuses answers `sheet_file`.
  - Every other key is ONE commit "Edit": one undo step and an `ev.edited`. The answer is the items read back in the order asked, with `renamed` and `replaced`. The frame queues the set with a fresh `/tmp/cc-schitems/<n>/` for its record (`items.json`), waits for the apply queue to empty and the record, and removes the folder whatever the answer: `not_applied` when no record came within about 5 s or KiCad applied the set to fewer items than asked.
- **`sch.image.place`** takes a picture, `bytes` a PNG or a JPEG (at most 4 MiB: `too_large`) and `name` its file name (1 to 255 characters, no slash, backslash or control character), writes it into a fresh `/tmp/cc-schimage/<n>/`, hands it to KiCad's own image tool and answers `{}` once it hangs on the pointer. The reader's click places it as KiCad's one undo step (then an `ev.edited`); Esc drops it. While it hangs every gated op answers `busy`, as after `place`. `not_applied` when KiCad cannot read the picture, `busy` when its tool did not take it within about 10 s.
- The fixture `tests/fixtures/items-sch/` (a root with one of every kind and a sheet `sub`, and a spare sheet file) is read by `tests/e2e/sch-items.spec.ts` (its run 15 half on cc25).
