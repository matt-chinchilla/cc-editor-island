### Board commands: the board's own windows asked of the host, and board corners (`vertices`)

Island `v0.2.3-cc25`, in a `pcb` frame (unit m3-i). Our engine from run 15 asks the host in place of three of KiCad's board windows, through the prompt host (Prompts, above), and reads and moves a board item's corners with no window.

The board asks and their records (lengths in millimeters, layers KiCad's canonical names):

- `convert` (KiCad's conversion settings, before Create Polygon, Zone, Rule Area or Lines from a selection): `{ strategies, strategy, gap, lineWidth, deleteOriginals }`. `strategies` the choices KiCad's dialog shows, in its order, from `copyLineWidth`, `centerline`, `boundingHull` (`[]` for Create Lines from a polygon, which shows none); `strategy` the selected one, which may be one the dialog hides; `gap` and `lineWidth` (the bounding hull's) 0 or more. Answer the same keys but `strategies`, each optional; a strategy not in `strategies` is refused with field `strategy`. A zone or a rule area made by the conversion asks `zoneSettings` after it, and is made with the conversion's last settings.
- `outset` (Create Outsets): `{ outsetDistance, roundCorners, useSourceLayers, useSourceWidths, layer, layers, lineWidth, gridRounding, deleteSourceItems }`. `layer` the selected layer, `layers` the layers the dialog offers (the board's enabled layers in KiCad's order), `gridRounding` the rounding grid or `null` when rounding is off. Answer the same keys but `layers`, each optional: `layer` one of `layers`; `gridRounding` `null` turns rounding off, a length over 0 turns it on; a `lineWidth` of 0 or less is refused with field `lineWidth` and KiCad's sentence "Line width must be a positive value."
- `zoneSettings` (a NEW zone: one drawn, one made by Convert, Duplicate Zone onto Layer; an existing zone's properties stay `ev.boardProperties` for copper and `ev.boardItem` for the other kinds): `{ kind, sheet }`. `kind` `copper` (the board editor only: a new copper zone in the footprint editor shows KiCad's window), `nonCopper` or `ruleArea`. For `copper` the `sheet` is the copper area of `board.item.get` (`kind: "zone"`, `locked`, `name`, `net`, `layers`, `priority`, `clearance`, `minWidth`, `padConnection`, `thermalGap`, `spokeWidth`, `netChoices`) with `uuid` `""`; for the other two it is the `zone` item record of `fpedit.items.get` (`ruleArea` false and true) with `uuid` `""`. Answer `{ set }`: for `copper` `board.item.set`'s zone keys plus `layers` (a non empty list of copper layers the board enables, as Duplicate onto a layer needs), each refused by its own key as `field`; for the other two the zone record's set keys. A canceled prompt makes no zone.

A record of any other shape is not taken, and KiCad's own window shows as before.

- **`board.vertices.get`** (exactly `{ uuid }`, any case): the item's corners as KiCad's vertex editor lists them: `kind` `copper` (a copper zone), `nonCopper` (a zone on no copper layer), `ruleArea` or `polygon` (a polygon shape); `outlines` every closed line of the item, each outline followed by its holes, `hole` `-1` for the outline itself and else the hole's index in that outline, each at least three corners, in board millimeters (no origin moved). A read: nothing changes and it is answered during a `board.check`.
- **`board.vertices.set`** (exactly `{ uuid, outline, hole, index, x, y }`): moves ONE corner to `(x, y)` (mm, within 50,000), as KiCad's vertex editor commits one ("Edit Vertex"): one undo step, a zone unfilled and marked for refill. The corner must be one the item holds (read first: `bad_args` names the missing outline, hole or corner, and nothing is sent). Answered at once with the item read back, as `board.vertices.get` answers it; `not_applied` when the engine refused the move or the read back does not hold the corner there. Moving a corner onto itself makes no undo step. An `ev.edited` follows.
- Failures of both: `bad_args` (args of another shape), `unsupported` (in another frame, "board corners are read in a pcb frame", or on an engine without `kicadBoardVerticesGet` or `kicadBoardVerticesSet`, named), `not_ready` (no board open), `busy` (a load parked, a popup menu, a dialog or a prompt up, or a placed item on the pointer; `board.vertices.set` also while a `board.check` runs), `not_found` (the board holds no zone, rule area or polygon of that uuid; `message` the uuid).
- **Held**: `ev.ready` lists `vertices` in a `pcb` frame exactly when the engine has both exports. While it is listed, KiCad's Edit Corners (`pcbnew.InteractiveEdit.editVertices`, which would open KiCad's vertex editor pane) is held: the pane never opens, the frame sends `ev.held`, and the host opens its board form's Corners block over `board.vertices.get`. The footprint editor's engine reads no corners, so `vertices` is never listed in an `fpedit` frame and the action keeps no door there. On an older engine (run 14 and before) the cap is absent, the action keeps no door in our menu, and both ops answer `unsupported` naming `kicadBoardVerticesGet`.
