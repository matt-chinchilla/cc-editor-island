### settings.read
The setup of ANOTHER design, read by KiCad and applied to nothing, in a `sch` or `pcb` frame with a document open (island `v0.2.3-cc25`, on an engine with `kicadSettingsRead` and `kicadCollabBusy`, engine run 15; a host gates it on `ev.ready` `caps` listing `settingsRead`, listed by `sch` and `pcb` frames only on an engine with both). The args are exactly `{ files, sections }`: `files` 1 to 3 of exactly `{ name, bytes }`, the file's plain name (1 to 255 bytes of UTF-8, no slash, backslash or control character, never `.` or `..`, a stem before its extension) and its bytes (a `Uint8Array`), exactly one `.kicad_pro`, at most one `.kicad_pcb` and at most one `.kicad_dru`, 25 MiB together; `sections` 1 to 64 unique names of the sections that travel, KiCad's Import Settings options: the board's `layers`, `stackup`, `finish`, `zoneHatchOffsets`, `textDefaults`, `zoneDefaults`, `boardFormatting`, `constraints`, `predefinedSizes`, `teardrops`, `tuningPatterns`, `netClasses`, `componentClasses`, `tuningProfiles`, `customRules`, `checkSeverities`, and the schematic's `schFormatting`, `annotation`, `fieldTemplates`, `bomPresets`, `ercSeverities`, `pinMap`, `netClasses`, `busAliases`, `textVariables` (the page, the variants, the embedded files, the drawing sheet and the preferences never travel). The checks, in order: the args (`bad_args`: no project file, a name with a folder, two files of a kind, another extension, a section that does not travel); the frame (`unsupported` "settings.read runs in a sch or pcb frame"); the engine (`not_ready`, `unsupported` naming the first missing export); a document open (`not_ready`); a load parked, a popup or a dialog up, or an item hanging off the pointer (`busy`), and during a check or an import.

The frame writes the files under a fresh `/tmp/cc-settings-read/<n>/`, outside the project folder (the rules file beside the board under the board's stem, as KiCad reads `<board>.kicad_dru`; no rules file without a board), and queues the read: KiCad loads the other project with its own project loader and its board with its own board reader, reads each asked section with the same readers `settings.get` uses, and unloads them again. Nothing of the open design changes: no undo step, no `ev.edited`, the design's own `settings.get` answers unchanged. The folder is removed whatever the answer.

The answer is `{ sections, missing }`: each asked name either a key of `sections`, its value in the shape `settings.get` answers for that section, or in `missing`, a section the other design does not hold or this frame does not read (a board section in a `sch` frame or with no board given, a schematic section in a `pcb` frame, `customRules` with no rules file). A host applies what it takes with `settings.set`, one section at a time, asking each frame for its own. `unreadable` when KiCad could not load the project or the board (`message` KiCad's first line, the run folder taken out, at most 512 characters); `not_applied` when the engine refused the read or no record came within 60 s; `island_error` for a record of another shape.
