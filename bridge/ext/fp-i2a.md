### The footprint editor's items: fpedit.items.get, set, add, remove

Island `v0.2.3-cc25`, in an `fpedit` frame, on an engine with `kicadFpEditItemsGet`, `kicadFpEditItemsSet`, `kicadFpEditItemsAdd`, `kicadFpEditItemsRemove` and `kicadFpEditState` (engine run 15); a host gates them on `ev.ready` `caps` listing `fpedit`. In every other frame each answers `unsupported` ("a footprint edits in an fpedit frame"); on an engine without the exports, `unsupported` naming the first missing one. The checks run in this order, each before any effect: the args (`bad_args`), the frame, the engine (`not_ready`, `unsupported`), a load parked or a popup menu or a dialog up (`busy`), a footprint open (`no_footprint`).

- **`fpedit.items.get`**: `uuids` 1 to 4,096, `kinds` 1 to 11, `layers` 1 to 256 KiCad layer names, none twice; every filter given must hold (an item is on a layer when its layer or one of its layers is listed). The footprint's own items in KiCad's order (fields, pads, then the drawings; a group or a table cell is never listed), each with its `kind` and its `uuid`. `not_found` names the first uuid the footprint does not hold. Answered during a check (a read).
- **`fpedit.items.set`**: `uuids` as above, every one held (`not_found` names the first), all of one kind (`bad_args`), and `set`, a non empty partial of the keys that kind takes (below; never `kind` or `uuid`), each in its type and bounds, and only keys every item has (a text inside a footprint has `keepUpright`, one outside a footprint `locked`; a picture's `grayscale` is an action, not a key it holds): `bad_args` names the key. A shape keeps its `shape`, a dimension its `dimType`, a zone its `ruleArea` (`bad_args` "a <kind> keeps its <key>"); a dimension's `textPos` needs its `textPosition` `manual` (a leader's always is). A set never holds `kind`, so a barcode's type is its key `symbology`. A pad's `copper`, each `layerShapes` entry's `copper` and its `teardrop` are partial; `primitives` and `layerShapes` replace the list; a `backdrill` or `postMachining` side is whole. KiCad's own dialog rules refuse what the dialog refuses (`bad_args`: a hole on an SMD pad, a trapezoid delta on both axes, a through hole pad with no hole, a padstack mode on an SMD pad, a field's name, a table's rows, columns, widths or heights other than they are, an empty text) and reset what the dialog resets (a circle's size squared and its offset dropped, a rounded rectangle's ratio, a type's default layers and hole). ONE undo step "Edit" over every item. The answer is the items read back once every key shows, or once the step shows when the dialog's rules reset a key given; past about 5 s `not_applied`.
- **`fpedit.items.add`**: 1 to 512 new items, each its `kind`, its uuid (the host's own, lower case, none the footprint holds: `bad_args` "<uuid> is taken"), the keys its kind needs and any other key a set of its kind takes: a pad nothing (it starts from the pad defaults, at the origin), a field `name` (one no field holds) and `layer` (its text may be empty), a text `text` and `layer`, a text box `text`, `layer` and `points`, a table `layer`, `colWidths` and `rowHeights` (1 to 64 each; `cells`, when given, rows by columns), a barcode `layer` (`symbology` optional, KiCad's QR otherwise), a shape `layer`, `shape` and `points` (`cornerRadius` on a rectangle only), a dimension `dimType`, `layer`, `start` and `end` (not the same point; the keys of its type only), a zone `layers` and `points` (always a rule area: `ruleArea`, when given, true), a picture `layer` and `png` (base64 of a PNG, at most 4 MiB decoded: a JPEG is converted first), a point `layer`. ONE undo step "Add"; the answer is the new items read back.
- **`fpedit.items.remove`**: 1 to 4,096 uuids, every one held (`not_found`), never the Reference or the Value field (`bad_args`); ONE undo step "Delete"; the answer once none reads back.

The records (lengths in mm, angles in degrees, positions relative to the footprint's origin, a string at most 1,024 characters unless named, a layer KiCad's canonical name; any other shape is `island_error`):

- `pad`: `number`, `pinName`, `pinType`, `type` (`smd`, `th`, `npth`, `conn`, `aperture`), `x`, `y`, `angle`, `locked`, `padstack` (`normal`, `front_inner_back`, `custom`), `copper` (PadShape, the front or only shape), `layerShapes` (`[{ layer, copper }]`, every unique layer but F.Cu, empty while normal), `drill` (`{ shape: "circle" | "oval", size: [w, h] }` or null), `layers`, `dieLength`, `dieDelay` (ps, 0 to 2,000), `clearance`, `maskMargin`, `pasteMargin` (each a number or null), `pasteRatio` (-1 to 1 or null), `zoneConnection` (`inherit`, `none`, `thermal`, `solid`), `customZoneShape` (`outline`, `convexhull`), `thermalSpokeWidth`, `thermalGap` (each or null), `thermalAngle`, `property` (`none`, `bga`, `fiducial_global`, `fiducial_local`, `testpoint`, `heatsink`, `castellated`, `mechanical`, `pressfit`), `unconnectedLayers` (`keep_all`, `remove_except_start_and_end`, `remove_all`), `teardrop` (`{ enabled, useTwoTracks, preferZoneConnection, maxLength, maxWidth, lengthRatio, widthRatio, curved, sizeFilterRatio }`), `backdrill` (`{ front, back }`, each `{ size, end }` with `end` a copper layer or null, or null), `postMachining` (`{ front, back }`, each `{ mode: "none" | "countersink" | "counterbore", size, depth, angle }`).
- PadShape: `shape` (`circle`, `rect`, `oval`, `trapezoid`, `roundrect`, `chamfered_rect`, `custom`), `size`, `offset`, `delta`, `roundRatio` and `chamferRatio` (0 to 0.5), `chamferCorners` (`tl`, `tr`, `bl`, `br`), `anchor` (`circle`, `rect`), `primitives` (a custom shape's only, at most 512, each `{ shape: "line" | "arc" | "rect" | "circle" | "polygon" | "bezier", points, width, filled }` in the pad's own coordinates: a line or a rectangle its two ends, an arc start, middle and end, a circle its center and a point on it, a curve its four controls, a polygon three points or more).
- `field`: `name`, `text` (one line), `layer`, `x`, `y`, `angle`, `size` (`[w, h]`, 0.001 to 250), `thickness`, `autoThickness`, `bold`, `italic`, `mirrored`, `hJustify` (`left`, `center`, `right`), `vJustify` (`top`, `center`, `bottom`), `knockout`, `font` (KiCad's name; "KiCad Font" its stroke font), `visible`, `keepUpright`. `text`: a field's keys with `name` "" and no `visible`; inside a footprint one line and `keepUpright`, outside one (the board's) lines and `locked`.
- `textbox`: `text` (lines), `points` (a box square to the axes its two corners, settable; a turned box its four, read only), the text style keys of a field (`layer` to `font`), `locked`, `border`, `borderWidth`, `borderStyle` (`solid`, `dash`, `dot`, `dash_dot`, `dash_dot_dot`); after a set the box grows to its text as KiCad's dialog grows it.
- `table`: `x`, `y`, `layer`, `locked`, `cells` (rows of texts as KiCad's grid shows them, a covered cell null), `borderExternal`, `borderHeader`, `borderWidth` (null while both are off), `borderStyle`, `rowSeparators`, `colSeparators`, `separatorsWidth`, `separatorsStyle`, `rows`, `cols`, `colWidths`, `rowHeights` (1 to 64 each; read only on a set: given back equal, or refused).
- `barcode`: `text` (one line, at most 4,096 characters), `layer`, `locked`, `x`, `y`, `width`, `height`, `textSize`, `angle`, `knockout`, `margin` (`[x, y]`), `showText`, `symbology` (`code39`, `code128`, `datamatrix`, `qr`, `microqr`), `errorCorrection` (`L`, `M`, `Q`, `H`). A text that yields no symbol is refused.
- `shape`: `layer`, `shape` (`segment`, `rect`, `arc`, `circle`, `polygon`, `bezier`), `points` (the counts of a primitive's, a segment's two ends; a polygon its main outline), `width`, `style`, `filled` (`fill` as a yes or no; given beside `fill` it agrees), `fill` (`none`, `solid`, `hatch`, `reverse_hatch`, `cross_hatch`; a segment or an arc never filled), `locked`, `cornerRadius` (a rectangle's only, at most half its short side), `hasSolderMask`, `solderMaskMargin` (a length or null for the board's). A segment or an arc, or an unfilled closed shape, needs a width.
- `dimension`: `dimType` (`aligned`, `orthogonal`, `center`, `radial`, `leader`), `layer`, `start`, `end`, `height` and `extensionHeight` (aligned and orthogonal only), `orientation` (`horizontal`, `vertical`; orthogonal only), `leaderLength` (radial only), `textBorder` (`none`, `rectangle`, `circle`, `roundrect`; leader only), `override`, `value`, `prefix`, `suffix`, `arrowDirection` (`inward`, `outward`), `units` (`inch`, `mils`, `mm`, `auto`), `unitsFormat` (`none`, `bare`, `paren`), `precision` (an index 0 to 9 of KiCad's list, not a digit count), `suppressZeroes`, `textPosition` (`outside`, `inline`, `manual`), `textPos`, `keepAligned`, `angle`, `size`, `thickness`, `bold`, `italic`, `hJustify`, `mirrored`, `font`, `lineThickness`, `arrowLength`, `extensionOffset`, `locked`.
- `zone`: `ruleArea`, `name`, `layers`, `locked`, `points` (its main outline; a set replaces it only on a zone of one outline with no hole, 3 points or more), `borderStyle` (`none`, `edge`, `full`), `hatchPitch` (0.1 to 2 on a set); a rule area also `noTracks`, `noVias`, `noPads`, `noCopperPour`, `noFootprints`, `placement`, `placementSource` (`sheet`, `componentClass`, `group`, `designBlock`), `placementName`. A zone of the footprint on no copper layer has no fill keys.
- `image` (a reference picture): `x`, `y` (its center), `scale`, `layer`, `bytes` (the PNG's own size, as given or as the file holds it), `png` (base64) while that is at most 64 KiB, and `locked` in the board frame only. A set takes `grayscale: true` (KiCad's one shot Convert to grayscale, which changes the picture) and never `png`; a scaled side under 15 pixels is refused.
- `point`: `x`, `y`, `layer`, `size` (over 0), `locked`.
