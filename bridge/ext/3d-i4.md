### board.models, board.setModels and board.embedModels

The board's 3D model lines, for the host's Models window (island `v0.2.3-cc25`, on an engine with `kicadBoardModels`, `kicadBoardSetModels` and `kicadBoardEmbedModels`, engine run 15; a host gates them on `ev.ready` `caps` listing `boardModels`, listed by a `pcb` frame only). On an older engine the cap is absent and each op answers `unsupported` naming the export. A `sch` frame answers `unsupported`.

`ModelLine` is `{ path, offset: [x, y, z], rotate: [x, y, z], scale: [x, y, z], opacity, show, found }`: `path` as the board writes it (1 to 1,024 characters, no control character), the vectors three finite numbers, `opacity` 0 to 1, `show` a boolean, and `found` where the engine finds the body at that moment: `embedded` (a `kicad-embed://` name the footprint or the board holds), `project` (a file of the project folder), `staged` (a body the engine fetched under `/pcbjam/3dmodels/`) or `missing`.

- `board.models` answers the engine's read as it is: every footprint with its uuid, its reference, its lines in order and its kinds (`notInPos` and `dnp` for the board's current variant), and the board's own embedded files (`type` KiCad's word: `model`, `font`, `worksheet`, `datasheet` or `other`; `bytes` decompressed). It is answered during a `board.check`.
- `board.setModels` replaces the lines of 1 to 4,096 footprints (no footprint twice, uuids lowercase, at most 64 lines each, every key of a line given and no other, `found` left out) as ONE undo step pushed under `label`. The answer comes once the apply queue is empty and every footprint's lines read back as sent: `changed` counts the footprints whose lines the request changed. An `ev.edited` follows. `not_found` names the first uuid that is no footprint's; nothing is changed then.
- `board.embedModels` keeps the bodies of 1 to 4,096 lines (`index` 0 to 63 in its footprint's lines, no line twice) inside the board, as KiCad's Embed 3D models does. The island reads each body itself before anything is written: a `${KICAD*_3DMODEL_DIR}` line through the model store (a VRML line's STEP twin), a `${KIPRJMOD}` or relative line from the project folder, a VRML line with its STEP sibling (`.step` or `.stp`) kept beside it, or the sibling alone when the VRML file is absent. The bodies go into one fresh `/tmp/cc-embed/<n>/`, each under its own file name (a second body of the same name takes `-2`, `-3` before its extension), and the engine keeps them as ONE commit "Keep models inside the board", each line then naming its `kicad-embed://` file. The answer, once the apply queue is empty: `embedded` the files kept, `names` their names (sorted, distinct), `bytes` their size. The folder is removed after every answer. `bad_args` names a line that is not on the board, already inside it, or whose body the island cannot read (a library line the model store does not hold, a project file the folder lacks; a line's `found` `missing` alone refuses nothing, since the engine reads every library line missing until it has fetched the body) (`message` "<reference> <path>", or "<uuid> line <index>"); `too_large` names a body past 25 MiB, or the op when the bodies pass 512 MiB together; nothing is written then. `not_applied` when the engine refuses the embedding or its apply queue does not empty within 30 s.
- The checks, in order, for each: the args (`bad_args`), the frame, the engine (`not_ready`, `unsupported`), a board (`not_ready`), busy as for a board op (and `busy` while the engine cannot read the board's models).
- Held in a `pcb` frame under `boardModels`, each sending `ev.held` and opening nothing: KiCad's Update 3D model paths (`pcbnew.GlobalEdit.migrate3DModels`) and Embed 3D models (`pcbnew.EditorControl.collect3DModels`); the host's Models window opens in their place.
