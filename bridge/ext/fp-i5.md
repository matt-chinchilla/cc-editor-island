### graphics.import
Brings a DXF or SVG drawing into the open document by KiCad's own importer, with no window of KiCad's (island `v0.2.3-cc25`, on an engine with `kicadImportGraphics`, engine run 15; a host gates it on `caps` listing `importGraphics`, listed by a `sch` or `pcb` frame on an engine with `kicadImportGraphics` and `kicadCollabBusy`, by a `symedit` frame with the symbol editor's exports too, by an `fpedit` frame with the footprint editor's). It is the body of KiCad's Import Graphics dialogs (the board and footprint editors' and the schematic and symbol editors'), the half picked by the frame.

The args are `name` (the file's name, 1 to 255 bytes of UTF-8, no slash, backslash or NUL), `bytes` (a `Uint8Array` of 1 byte to 25 MB), `place` (`at` or `pointer`), and, all optional: `layer` (a board layer's canonical name, `Dwgs.User`, `F.SilkS`; absent, the frame's active layer; a `sch` or `symedit` frame ignores it), `scale` (0.0001 to 10,000, default 1), `origin` (`[x, y]` in mm, each within 2,000, default `[0, 0]`), `lineWidth` (mm, above 0 and at most 100, or `null` for the importer's default; a DXF only, an SVG keeps its own widths), `dxfUnits` (absent: the file's own `$INSUNITS`), `group` (the items in one group), `fixDiscontinuities` and `tolerance` (join the ends of lines closer than `tolerance` mm, 0 to 100, default 1). Any other key or shape answers `bad_args`.

The checks, in order, before any effect: the args (`bad_args`); the frame (a `ds` frame answers `unsupported`); the engine (`not_ready`, `unsupported` naming the first missing export); a document open (`not_ready` in a `sch` or `pcb` frame, `no_symbol`, `no_footprint`); a load parked, a popup or a dialog up, or (`sch`, `pcb`) an item hanging off the pointer (`busy`); then the file's kind BY ITS CONTENT, never its name: a DXF when, past any `999` comments, its first group is `0` `SECTION`, an SVG when, past the XML declaration, comments and a doctype, its first element is `svg`; anything else answers `not_a_drawing`. The frame writes the drawing under a fresh `/tmp/cc-gfx/<n>/` with the extension of its kind (KiCad picks its reader by the extension), outside the project folder, and removes the folder once the importer has read it.

- `place: "at"`: the drawing lands at `origin` as ONE commit (one undo step and an `ev.edited`; one Ctrl+Z through `key.press` takes the whole drawing back); the answer comes once the apply queue is empty, else `not_applied` after about 5 s. In an `fpedit` frame the items join the open footprint (they save with it through `fpedit.take`), in a `symedit` frame the open symbol's current unit.
- `place: "pointer"`: the answer comes at once and the drawing hangs on the pointer in KiCad's move tool (R rotates it); a click places it as ONE commit, Esc leaves nothing and no undo step. The uuids name the items the click will place. In a `sch` or `pcb` frame the hanging drawing is joined to `place`'s watch: every gate answers `busy` until it ends, and a later `place` cancels it first.

The answer is `{ items, uuids, warnings }`: `items` the drawing's items (a group made by `group` is not counted and its uuid not listed), `uuids` their uuids (at most 4,096), `warnings` KiCad's importer messages, one per line (at most 64, each cut to 1,024 characters). `unreadable` when KiCad read nothing it can place (`message` its warnings, or "KiCad read nothing it can place from this drawing"); `bad_args` "graphics.import: KiCad's importer refused it (a layer this document does not enable, or the document cannot take a drawing now)" when the engine refuses the call.

KiCad's own Import Graphics (Ctrl+Shift+F, its menu and toolbar items) opens nothing: under `importGraphics` a `pcb` frame holds `pcbnew.InteractiveDrawing.placeImportedGraphics` and a `sch` frame holds `eeschema.EditorControl.importGraphics`, each sending `ev.held { action }`; a `symedit` frame holds the latter already under `symedit`; an `fpedit` frame sends `ev.fpedit { ask: "graphics", uuid: "", isNew: false }` (cap `fpeditHeld`). The host opens its own Import a drawing page for each.

The fixtures `tests/fixtures/drawings/outline.dxf` (a closed polyline and an arc, mm) and `logo.svg` (two paths) import in all four frames (`tests/e2e/graphics-import.spec.ts`, engine run 15).
